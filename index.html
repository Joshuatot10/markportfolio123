Game Engine Pipeline

Mark 3D Production Workflow

A disciplined 5-step process ensuring game assets perform seamlessly without sacrificing visual quality.

Step 01
Step 01

Concept, Reference & Blockout (Blender)

Gathering mood boards for streetwear silhouettes or hard-surface logos. Creating initial low-poly blockouts in Blender to establish silhouette proportions and mesh topology flow.

Step 02
Step 02

High-Poly Sculpting & Seam UV Unwrapping

Sculpting high-frequency details like leather wrinkles, seams, and metal scratches. Marking UV seams precisely to maintain uniform Texel Density across UV islands.

Step 03
Step 03

PBR Texturing (Substance Painter & GIMP)

Baking high-to-low normal maps, AO, and curvature. Designing custom decals in GIMP, applying smart cloth/leather materials, and stenciling graphic prints in Substance Painter.

Step 04
Step 04

Optimization & Rigging Setup

Ensuring clean triangle count budgets, weight painting clothing meshes to game character skeletons, and generating LODs (Level of Detail) if required.

Step 05
Step 05

Game Engine Import (3D Modeling Blender / Roblox Studio)

Exporting optimized FBX meshes with baked PBR texture sets (Albedo, Normal, Roughness, Metallic, Emission) and verifying shader lighting inside game engine viewports.
